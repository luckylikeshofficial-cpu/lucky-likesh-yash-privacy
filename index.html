Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

Lucky Likesh Yash – All Tools ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Lucky Likesh Yash – All Tools Android application.

Information We Collect

The app provides creator, photo, PDF, calculator, student, and utility tools. Many of these tools process files or information directly on your device. We do not require you to create an account to use the app.

Information that you enter into tools, such as text, images, PDFs, notes, calculator values, or other files, may be processed locally on your device depending on the tool you use.

Photos, Videos and Files

When you select photos, videos, or PDF files, the app uses the permissions and file-selection features provided by Android to access the files needed for the requested operation.

Files selected for tools such as image conversion, resizing, compression, PDF creation, and related operations are processed for the requested functionality. We do not intentionally collect or sell the contents of your personal files.

Notes and User-Entered Content

If you use features such as Notes, the information you enter may be stored locally on your device so that the feature can function. We do not intentionally transmit your personal notes to our servers.

Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may collect and use information such as advertising identifiers, device information, and usage information to provide, personalize, measure, and improve advertising, subject to applicable Google policies and your available privacy choices.

For more information about how Google handles information for advertising, please review Google's privacy policies.

Third-Party Services

The app uses third-party services, including Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Internet and Network Access

Some features, such as advertising and internet speed testing, may require an internet connection. Network-related information may be processed by the relevant third-party service when required for the feature to work.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to avoid unnecessary collection of personal information. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's Privacy

The app is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

Your Choices

You can stop using the app or uninstall it at any time. You can also manage available Android permissions and advertising privacy settings through your device and Google settings.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact
